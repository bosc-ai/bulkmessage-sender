Why we built Weflux

WhatsApp is where Indian customers are. The tools weren't.

Over 500 million Indians use WhatsApp daily. For millions of SMBs, it's the primary way customers ask questions, check on orders, and get support.

But the tools to manage those conversations professionally - as a team, at scale, with proper templates and analytics - were either too expensive, built for Western markets, or required a developer to set up.

We built Weflux because we believe businesses deserve a WhatsApp platform that's fast to set up, priced fairly, and actually built around how modern teams work.

What Weflux is

Weflux is a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider built on the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, operated by Serves Technologies from Bengaluru, India. Businesses use it to run broadcast campaigns, work a shared team inbox on one WhatsApp number, automate the predictable messages, and keep a CRM that updates itself from the conversation.

The one thing we do differently

Meta charges businesses per message. Most providers add a margin to that rate and do not draw attention to it, because it is invisible on a monthly invoice and grows with your volume. By the time it is material, you have built your operations on the platform.

Weflux passes Meta's charges through at cost and shows Meta's own rate on every invoice line. We make money on the platform fee, which is a number you can see and compare, rather than on a spread you cannot.

That is a deliberately smaller business than the alternative. It is also the only version of this we wanted to run, and it is the reason customers with real volume move to us.

How we decide what to build

Three rules, which explain most of our product decisions and a fair number of our refusals.

Official API only. We do not offer, and will not build, anything that automates the WhatsApp app. Those tools work until the number is banned, and the ban is usually permanent. Any provider offering both an official and an unofficial mode is telling you something about how they will treat your number.

Say what it costs before it costs it. Windows closing, template-only states, opt-outs, permanent deletes. The interface warns in advance and in plain words, and asks twice for anything unrecoverable.

Never show a control that cannot work. Messenger and Instagram cannot be messaged first and have no templates. Rather than grey out a button that could never function, those surfaces show only what the channel actually supports. A disabled control that can never be enabled is a support ticket with extra steps.

What we are not

We would rather lose a deal at the first email than at month three.

  • Not a general helpdesk. If most of your volume is email and WhatsApp is a side channel, a helpdesk with a WhatsApp connector will serve you better.
  • Not a full ticketing system. Conversations, assignment, notes and lead stages, not SLA policies, escalation matrices and CSAT surveys.
  • Not a multi-channel suite. WhatsApp first, with Facebook Messenger and Instagram alongside it. No SMS, no email.
  • Not certified. We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001. The security page lists the controls we actually operate, and we will tell you when that changes.

Who we build for

Small and mid-size Indian businesses whose sales and support genuinely live in a WhatsApp inbox for eight hours a day. D2C brands, clinics, coaching centres, service firms, and the agencies that run WhatsApp on their behalf. Mixed technical confidence, often working from a mid-range Android phone on a patchy connection, switching between phone and laptop through the same shift.

They are not evaluating software. They are clearing a queue. Success for us is a team that stops using their personal WhatsApp because the shared inbox is faster.
