How this comparison was made

Every figure below comes from the provider's own public pricing page, read on 6 September 2026. Nothing is taken from third-party comparison blogs, which are frequently out of date and often written by whoever benefits. Pricing changes: check their current page before deciding, and if you find something here that is wrong, tell us at hello@weflux.in and we will correct it.

We have not included feature-by-feature tick lists. Every provider here is an official Meta BSP running on the same Cloud API, so the messages travel identically and most feature lists converge. What genuinely differs is how you are charged.

Where AiSensy is genuinely strong

Starting here, because pretending otherwise would waste your time.

AiSensy's pricing page publishes Meta's Indian per-message rates directly: marketing ₹1.09, utility ₹0.145, authentication ₹0.145, service free. It states unlimited users with no per-agent charge. On the two things Weflux argues about most, pass-through billing and not metering seats, AiSensy takes the same position.

It also has a considerably larger integrations catalogue and is the more established brand in the Indian market. If you are choosing on ecosystem breadth, that is a real advantage and we are not going to talk you out of it.

The real difference: bundled or à la carte

Both companies pass Meta's charges through. The difference is what the subscription buys.

On AiSensy, the chatbot builder is a ₹2,500 a month add-on and the AI agent builder another ₹1,350. A business that wants automation, which is most businesses, is looking at the base plus roughly ₹2,500 to ₹3,850 a month in add-ons before messages.

On Weflux, automation flows are part of the plan. Scale is ₹4,999 a month with ten agents and automation included, and there is no separate charge to turn a chatbot on.

Which is better genuinely depends on you. If you want the inbox and broadcasts and no automation at all, AiSensy's unbundled base may cost less. If automation is the reason you are buying a WhatsApp platform, compare the bundle rather than the headline.

Where AiSensy is the better choice

  • You have a very large team. AiSensy's unlimited users beats a plan cap of ten. Above roughly ten agents, this alone may decide it.
  • You need a specific pre-built integration. Their catalogue is larger. If the tool you depend on is on their list and not ours, that is a practical answer.
  • You want the more established brand. AiSensy is bigger and better known in India. If procurement weighs vendor size, they will score higher, and that is a legitimate consideration.
  • You want marketing depth specifically. Their retargeting and click-to-WhatsApp ad tooling is more developed than ours today.

Where Weflux fits better

  • Automation is central and you want it in the plan rather than as a ₹2,500 a month add-on.
  • You want one number on the invoice rather than a base plus three add-ons to reconcile.
  • Your team is up to about ten people, where a plan cap costs you nothing.
  • You want a smaller company that will answer the phone. That cuts both ways and you should weigh it honestly.
