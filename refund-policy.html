Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear terms, transparent timelines, and hassle-free cancellations.

Last updated · 15 September 2026 · Effective for all Weflux customers.

1. Free trial

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No payment is collected during the trial period. If you do not choose to upgrade to a paid subscription, your account moves to the Free plan automatically at the end of 14 days. No charge is made, and no cancellation request is needed.

2. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time directly from Settings → Billing inside your Weflux dashboard at app.weflux.in, or by sending an email to hello@weflux.in.

Cancellation takes effect immediately at the end of your current billing period. You retain full access to all features and your data until that date. We do not require lock-in contracts, notice periods, or phone calls to cancel.

3. Refunds on monthly plans

If you cancel within 7 calendar days of your first paid charge, we will refund that subscription charge in full upon request.

After 7 days from the charge date, monthly subscriptions are non-refundable for the current active billing period, but your account will not be charged again for subsequent months.

4. Refunds on annual plans

Annual plans may be refunded on a pro-rata basis for unused full months, provided the refund request is submitted within 30 calendar days of purchase or renewal. After 30 days from purchase, annual plans are non-refundable.

5. Meta conversation charges

Charges billed to you for WhatsApp conversations by Meta Platforms, Inc. are passed through at exact cost with 0% markup. These conversation charges are consumed at the time of sending or receiving messages and are charged to us directly by Meta; therefore, Meta conversation charges are strictly non-refundable.

6. Refund processing timeline

Approved refunds are processed with the following explicit timelines:

  • Initiation: Approved refund requests are initiated by our billing team within 3 business days of approval.
  • Payment Gateway Credit: The refund is credited back to your original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI) within 5–7 business days.
  • Depending on your issuing bank or card network, additional settlement processing may take 2–3 business days.

7. Service failure & SLA

If the Weflux application is unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours due to an outage or fault entirely within our control, you may request a pro-rata service credit or refund for the affected duration.

8. How to request a refund

To request a refund or raise a billing question, email hello@weflux.in or support@weflux.in from your registered account email address with the subject line "Refund Request" and your workspace name. We acknowledge and respond to all billing inquiries within 2 business days.
