The WhatsApp green tick, and how to actually get it.

What the green tick actually is

The green tick, formally the Official Business Account badge, is a verification mark Meta places next to a business name on WhatsApp. It tells a recipient that Meta has confirmed this is a notable, authentic brand.

What it changes: your business name shows in the chat header and in the recipient's list even when they have not saved your number. Without it, an unsaved contact sees the phone number. That is the practical benefit, and for a consumer brand it is a real one.

What it does not change: your messaging limits, your delivery rates, your per-message cost, or what you are allowed to send. Those are governed by your quality rating and template categories, not by the badge.

Green tick and Business Verification are not the same thing

These get conflated constantly, including by providers who should know better.

What Meta actually looks for

The criterion is notability, and Meta's own guidance is consistent about what that means: your brand being covered by others, not talked about by you.

Counts: coverage in established news outlets, being the subject of articles rather than mentioned in passing, a Wikipedia entry, and a substantial organic presence that exists independently of your advertising.

Does not count: paid placements and sponsored articles, press releases you distributed, your own social following, your ad spend, your revenue, how long you have been trading, or which provider you use. No BSP has influence over this decision, and any that claims to is selling you something it does not control.

How to apply

  1. Complete Business Verification first. It is a prerequisite, and it is the one you are likely to pass.
  2. Set your display name properly. It must match how the public knows the brand and comply with WhatsApp's display name policy. A name that reads like a keyword string will be rejected on its own.
  3. Gather your evidence. Three to five links to genuine third-party coverage, from outlets a reviewer will recognise, ideally about the brand rather than mentioning it.
  4. Apply through Business Manager for the WhatsApp Business Account. We will help you prepare and submit it.
  5. Expect to wait, and possibly to be declined. Decline is the common outcome and is not a reflection on your business. You can reapply, and the sensible time to do so is after genuine new coverage, not a month later with the same links.

If you are declined

Most businesses are, and the honest answer is that the fix is not a better application. It is more third-party coverage, which is a PR problem rather than a WhatsApp one.

What is worth doing in the meantime: set a clear display name and profile photo, complete your business profile with a description, address and website, and use templates that identify you in the first line. An unsaved recipient seeing a well-formed profile and a message that says who you are gets most of the trust benefit the badge would have provided.

A warning about paid green tick services

There is a market in India for services promising a guaranteed green tick for a fee. Nobody outside Meta can guarantee this outcome. At best you are paying someone to submit a form you could submit yourself; at worst you are paying for fabricated press coverage, which is a policy violation that can cost you the account you are trying to decorate.
